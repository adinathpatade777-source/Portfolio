Story Studio

Script → ChatGPT images → Kokoro voiceover → animated video / Google Vids pack. No API keys.

1 Script & scenes

Write or paste your script. Blank lines split scenes; one long paragraph is split every 2 sentences.

2 Create & collect images from ChatGPT

Click Open in ChatGPT (prompt is pre-filled) or Copy it. Download the image ChatGPT makes, then drop it on the scene, click the scene and press Ctrl+V to paste, or bulk-upload all images (sorted by file name, filled into empty scenes).

3 Voiceover (Kokoro TTS – runs in your browser)

Kokoro is a free open-source voice model. It downloads once (~90 MB) and then runs offline on your device – no API, no key. English voices only; for Hindi/Marathi, upload your own recording per scene in step 2.

4 Animate & export

How to animate in Google Vids (no API exists for Vids)
  1. Download the Google Vids pack and unzip it.
  2. Open vids.google.com → Blank video.
  3. Upload images/ – add one scene per image (order = file number).
  4. Select each image → Animate (zoom / pan / fade) and set its duration from timeline.txt.
  5. Upload voiceover/full-voiceover.wav as the audio track (or each scene's WAV under its scene).
  6. Optional: paste script.txt into captions, then export.

Or skip Vids entirely – Export video renders the same animation here in your browser.
